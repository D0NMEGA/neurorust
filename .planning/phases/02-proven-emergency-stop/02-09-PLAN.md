<threat_model>
## Trust boundaries

This plan writes claims about numbers taken on a machine most readers will never see. Its threats
are about a reader believing something the evidence does not support. Nothing here runs on the rig,
nothing here takes input, and nothing here holds a secret.

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| a committed capture -> a published sentence | A reader acts on the sentence and will not open the raw capture. |
| a decomposed figure -> a reader's arithmetic | Publishing components beside a total invites netting them, which is what D-22 exists to prevent. |
| this phase -> a future reader quoting it | The headline number will outlive the context that bounds it unless the bounds travel with it. |

## STRIDE threat register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-2-62 | Spoofing | a figure quoted without the stand-in caveat | mitigate | The caveat is a numbered paragraph inside the figure's own document rather than a link, and task 3 makes "would a quoted maximum be true out of context" one of the five review questions. |
