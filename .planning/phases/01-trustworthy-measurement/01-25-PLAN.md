<objective>
Make `TracersQuiescent` establish what its name claims when the tracer is driven through
`rtla`'s own tracing instance rather than through the top-level controls.

Purpose: plan 01-18 widened this check from `current_tracer` alone to all four top-level controls,
because `events/enable`, `set_event` and `tracing_on` can each arm tracing without changing
`current_tracer`. `rtla` defeats all four at once: it creates its own instance under
`/sys/kernel/tracing/instances/` and drives osnoise there, so the top level reads `nop`, `0`,
empty and `0` while osnoise sampling threads spin at SCHED_FIFO on the measured cores.

This is not hypothetical. On 2026-09-06 a SIGTERMed `rtla` left its osnoise kthreads behind, and
the next capture, `measurements/2026-09-06-precision3591-screen-02`, lost 75 percent of its
cyclictest cycles with all six threads stalled at roughly 750000 us. That number is
`/sys/kernel/tracing/osnoise/runtime_us`: an osnoise thread spins for `runtime_us` of every
`period_us`, so orphans on CPUs 6 to 11 take three quarters of every period from anything sharing
those cores at the same priority. All fifteen preconditions passed on that run.

Success criterion 2 is that a third party can reproduce a run under documented conditions. An
assertion list that certifies a machine which is not quiet does not meet that. The current
mitigation is a guard inside `~/nr-arm.sh` on the rig, which is a runbook aid, does not travel
with this repository, and is not the harness mechanism.

**This extends the existing check rather than adding a sixteenth precondition.** Two reasons, in
order of weight. First, the check is already named `TracersQuiescent` and its documented job is
that no tracer is armed for a headline-series run; an osnoise thread sampling the measured cores
is an armed tracer, so a check that misses it is not establishing what its name claims. That is
exactly the framing of plan 01-18 ("Gate audit: every precondition establishes what its name
claims"), which widened this same check for this same reason, and following its precedent keeps
one concept in one place instead of splitting "is a tracer armed" across two checks a reader has
to remember to read together. Second, the count fifteen is quoted in `README.md`, in
`docs/measurement-protocol.md` twice, in `crates/cli/src/cmd/run.rs`'s module doc, in two tests in
`crates/cli/tests/run_pipeline.rs`, and in the acceptance criteria of unexecuted plans 01-13 and
01-15, which this replan is not permitted to rewrite. A sixteenth check would falsify all of them
at once, and several are not this plan's to correct.

Both new signals are readable without root, which matters: the check runs before the run acquires
any privilege.

Runs entirely on the macOS dev host, through `FixtureFacts`, exactly as every other precondition
is tested. No rig access.

Output: a precondition that cannot be passed by a machine with osnoise threads on the measured
cores, and the operator-side guard moved into version control.
</objective>
